# 0010. What started the beef is contested, and the game never resolves it

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-25

## Context

The **beef** between **Roundhill** and **Orinda** is the engine of the entire game and had
no stated origin. That is not a gap that stays empty: writers need something to reference,
and in the absence of a decision one will be invented ad hoc and then hardened by repetition
into canon nobody chose.

`CONTEXT.md` already constrains the answer. **Beef** is defined as *a condition that
persists and is inherited*, with *no agreed start date that everyone involved would name
the same way, and no terms under which it could be declared over*. **The cycle** is defined
as something that *outlives the people in it* and that *no participant controls*.

Three answers were on the table.

**Territory.** The first proposal, and the most conventional. Rejected on two counts. It is
ruled out by name — `CONTEXT.md` defines a **set** as constituted *not by rank, dues, or
territory*, and [ADR 0001](0001-tactical-agency-strategic-futility.md) forecloses expanding
territory and consolidating power. More seriously, a territorial beef is *winnable*: a
player can correctly reason that taking or holding some ground would end it. ADR 0001 must
then either allow that, which destroys its thesis, or refuse it, which reads as the game
cheating — and refusal is the precise failure ADR 0001 names as its trigger for revisiting
the whole design. Territory supplies terms, and the definition of beef says there are none.

**A canonical non-material cause** — a specific killing, years ago. Better, because it
cannot be taken back, and it was genuinely considered. Rejected because it gives the cycle
a beginning, and a thing with a beginning implies a thing with an end. It also makes the
beef *about* something, which means one side is owed and the other is at fault — and
`CONTEXT.md` requires the rival set to be *a mirror, not an antagonist faction*. Worst, it
hands the player a grievance to be righteous about. That props up the *I am defending*
belief ADR 0001 depends on the player holding, but props it up on solid ground, so the
ending arrives as a twist rather than as a recognition.

**Contested.** No agreed account, and none is ever confirmed.

## Decision

We will never resolve what started the beef.

Multiple incompatible accounts circulate. Territory is one of them — someone will say it
was a corner, someone else will say it was somebody's cousin, someone else will say it was
always like this. Both blocks tell it differently and neither is internally consistent. The
player hears several and is never told which is true, because none of them is designated
true.

**No scene, document, collectible, or line of dialogue ever adjudicates.** There is no
flashback depicting the origin and no character who turns out to know.

**This is not a mystery.** That distinction is the whole of the implementation risk. A
mystery has a hidden answer and rewards assembly; this has no answer, and the accounts must
be written to contradict rather than to layer into something solvable. If they can be
reconciled by an attentive player, this decision has failed and a canonical origin has been
created by accident.

Territory survives as one of the stories people tell. It is never a live stake, never
something held or taken, and never a thing the player can act on.

## Consequences

**Easier.** There is no inciting incident to write, and none to make convincing enough to
carry a campaign. The rival set stays a mirror rather than becoming a party at fault, which
is what `CONTEXT.md` already asks of it. Nothing can be resolved, so the question *why not
just settle it* never has a surface to attach to. And writers gain a free instrument:
which account a character believes is characterization, available in ambient dialogue at no
structural cost — which matters under
[ADR 0003](0003-choice-lives-in-systems-not-dialogue.md), where such instruments are scarce.

Most importantly, the player is retaliating over something with no solid ground under it
from the first hour. ADR 0001's thesis is therefore operating from the beginning rather
than being revealed at the end.

**Harder.** Audiences and press expect an origin, and some will read its absence as the
writing having failed to supply one rather than having declined to. The accounts must be
authored with real discipline — irreconcilable, not merely incomplete — and that is harder
than writing one true version. There is also no dramatic set-piece available at the point
where most games would put one.

**Foreclosed.** A canonical origin, in any form. Territory as a live stake. Any scene,
line, or flashback that settles the question. Any character who knows.

**Worth revisiting if.** Playtesting shows players treat the contradictions as a puzzle and
feel cheated when it does not resolve. The response is to make the accounts more obviously
irreconcilable so the absence of an answer reads as deliberate — not to supply one, which
would reintroduce every problem above.
