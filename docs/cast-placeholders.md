## Notes for whoever does the rename

- The current names cluster as a different demography than a block drawn from Parkway
  Gardens. If the mixed set is kept deliberately, the fiction needs to account for how a
  block-level set ended up looking this way — much cheaper to build in than to retrofit.
- **Connor and Collin collide** phonetically and visually. Under
  [ADR 0002](adr/0002-permanent-death-shrinking-set.md) the player must instantly tell
  eight people apart while deciding who rides on the back and who might not come home;
  two near-identical names actively work against that. Split them at rename time.
- Aim for eight names that are distinct on first syllable and distinct in silhouette when
  written, for the same reason.
